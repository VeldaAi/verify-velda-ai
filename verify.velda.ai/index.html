What this is, and what it is not

Google runs more than one check. They are not the same thing.

  • Brand check. Google looks at the name, logo, homepage, and privacy link people see when they sign in with Google. This is the check these prices cover.
  • Sensitive-scope check. If the app reads things like Calendar or sends Gmail, Google reviews that use as well. It takes longer. A short demo video is usually required. This is a different review from the brand check.
  • Restricted-scope check. Broad access, such as all of Gmail or all of Drive, can also require a yearly security review by an outside lab (often called CASA). Google does not charge that lab fee. The lab does. That fee is not included in $100, $149, or $249. If your app needs it, we say so before any extra cost. We do not sign you up for a lab.

Google’s own timing notes, which are not promises: the automatic brand check often finishes in a few minutes, and a person reviewing the brand usually takes 2–3 business days. A sensitive-scope review can take about 10 business days. A restricted-scope review is listed as about 6 weeks, plus the lab. Google says those times vary. Sources are linked on the done-for-you page.

Privacy

What this site collects, and what we will not take.

What you can send us

The start form asks for your name, email, app name, homepage link, privacy-policy link, Google Cloud project ID, which Google data the app uses, the plan you want, whether you added our Google account, and an optional note.

That is enough to match a Stripe payment and to open the right Google Cloud project. The homepage must be a public https:// page on a domain you control. The privacy policy must be on that same domain.

Do not send passwords, card numbers, bank details, API keys, access tokens, refresh tokens, OAuth client secrets, or private keys. The form does not ask for them. Email does not want them either. Stripe collects the card on Stripe’s own page. We do not receive the card number.

Who else receives the form

  • Formspree receives the start form. The form posts to https://formspree.io/f/xgaaqljw. Formspree then emails it to us. Formspree’s own privacy policy applies to that delivery.
  • Stripe receives the payment when you use a Pay button. Stripe processes the card. We see the Stripe receipt (amount, email, and payment status), not the card number.

We use the form and the receipt to do the work you paid for, to email you, and to refund a done-for-you fee when the refund rule says so. We do not sell the form contents.

Google project access

If you add getappverified@gmail.com as OAuth Config Editor, that role can edit the Google sign-in settings and can also view OAuth client secrets that are stored in that Google Cloud project. We use the role to do the verification steps you hired us for. Remove the role when we email you that the work is finished. Steps are on the done-for-you page.

How to ask about your information

Email getappverified@gmail.com. This page does not list a postal address. We are not publishing one here because we are not inventing one.

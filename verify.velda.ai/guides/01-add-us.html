Guide 01: Add Us to Your Google Project

Your Google project = your app's settings area at Google. About 2 minutes.

← Back to intake form

Follow these steps with the Google account that owns your app. When you're done, reply to us with the line at the bottom.

  1. Sign in with the Google account that owns your app, then open:

    https://console.cloud.google.com/iam-admin/iam

  2. Click the project name at the top left and pick your app's project.

  3. In that list, copy your project's ID (looks like my-app-123456). Save it for your reply.

  4. Click Grant access. In the New principals box (where you type an email), paste: getappverified@gmail.com

  5. Click Select a role, type Owner, and click Owner (Legacy). It's fine that it says Legacy. Ignore the note about Admin.

  6. Click Save. If Google asks about sending an invitation, say yes.

    Button label not confirmed — look for Save / Send invitation / Confirm.

That's it. We accept the invitation on our side. "Pending" is normal.

Common mistakes

  • ❌ Picking Viewer, Editor, Reader, or Writer. It must be Owner (Legacy).
  • ❌ Wrong project. Check the name at the top.
  • ❌ Typing the email. Paste it.

Details (optional)
  • No Grant access button? You're signed in with an account that isn't an Owner. Switch to the account that owns the project.
  • Never send us your password. We don't need it.
  • The invitation: Google only lets someone become Owner through an email invitation they accept. Google sends it to getappverified@gmail.com and we accept it; you do nothing. Until then the page may say "Invitation sent. Pending acceptance."
  • Why Owner? Google requires an Owner or Editor to verify your domain, and only Owners can submit verification. Google contacts Owners about it.
  • Google calls this page "IAM" (its people-and-permissions list).
  • Removing us later: this page → pencil next to our email → Delete. Guide 05 walks you through it.

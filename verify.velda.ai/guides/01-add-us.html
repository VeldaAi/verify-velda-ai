Add us to your Google project

About 2 minutes. You stay the owner. We never ask for your password.

Do this only if you bought done-for-you, or you are about to. DIY buyers do not need to add us.

Add OAuth Config Editor. Do not add Owner.

Google’s permission list gives oauthconfig.verification.submit to the role named OAuth Config Editor. That is the smallest role we found that can edit the sign-in screen and submit the check. Owner can also delete the project, change billing, and manage every other product. We do not need Owner.

Google marks OAuth Config Editor as Beta. If the role box does not offer it, stop and email us. Do not switch to Owner unless we have confirmed the smaller role is missing and you agree in a reply. Source: OAuthConfig roles (page updated 4 November 2026).

This role can see OAuth client secrets stored in the project. That is still much smaller than Owner. Do not also email those secrets to us.

Steps

Google moves buttons. The words below match Google’s IAM help. If a word is missing, stop and email a screenshot of the whole screen. Do not click a nearby button that only sounds similar.

  1. Sign in as the Google account that owns the app. Open the IAM page.

  2. At the top left, open the project picker. Select the app’s project. Copy the project ID (it looks like my-app-123456). You will paste it into the start form.

  3. Click Grant access. In the principals box, paste exactly:

    getappverified@gmail.com

    Paste it. Do not retype it.

  4. Click Select a role. Type OAuth Config Editor. Pick OAuth Config Editor. Do not pick Owner, Editor, Viewer, or a role with “Legacy” in the name.

  5. Click Save. If Google asks to send an invitation, say yes. “Pending” is normal until we accept it.

Easy mistakes

  • Wrong project. Check the name in the top bar before you save.
  • Picking Owner “to be safe”. That is more access than this job needs.
  • A typo in the email. Paste getappverified@gmail.com.

When we are done

We will email you to remove us. The clicks are on the done-for-you page. You can remove us sooner any time. If you do that before we finish, the done-for-you refund exception applies.

If there is no Grant access button

You are probably signed in with an account that is not an owner of this project. Switch to the account that owns it. We will not ask you to hand that account’s password to us.

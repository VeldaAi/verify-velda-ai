Add us to your Google project

About 2 minutes. You stay the owner. We never ask for your password.

Do this only if you bought done-for-you, or you are about to. DIY buyers do not need to add us.

Add OAuth Config Editor. Do not add Owner.

Google does not require our email to be Owner. To prove a domain, Google’s sensitive-scope page says to use a Google account that is an Owner or an Editor of the Cloud project. That account should be yours. It does not have to be the only Owner, and it does not have to be our email.

Google’s permission list gives oauthconfig.verification.submit to the role named OAuth Config Editor. That is the smallest role we found that can edit the sign-in screen and submit the check. The broad Owner role can also delete the project and change billing. The broad Editor role can change most of the project. We do not need either of those for our email.

Google marks OAuth Config Editor as Beta. If Google says the address must be associated with an active Google Account, or if OAuth Config Editor does not appear in the role box, stop and email verify@velda.ai. Do not switch to Owner. Source: OAuthConfig roles (page updated 4 November 2026).

This role can see OAuth client secrets stored in the project. That is still much smaller than Owner. Do not also email those secrets to us.

Steps

Google moves buttons. The words below match Google’s IAM help. If a word is missing, stop and email a screenshot of the whole screen. Do not click a nearby button that only sounds similar.

  1. Sign in as the Google account that owns the app. Open the IAM page.

  2. At the top left, open the project picker. Select your app’s project. The picture shows a demo project named Demo App. Yours will have your own name.

  3. In that project list, copy the ID. It is in the ID column, next to the name. Paste it into the start form. The picture shows a demo ID, centered-kiln-513717-v1. Yours will be different. Do not copy the demo ID.

  4. Click Grant access. Under Add principals, click the box named New principals. Paste this email:

    verify@velda.ai

    Paste it. Do not retype it.

  5. Under Assign roles, open Role. The box may say Select a role before you pick. Type OAuth Config Editor. Pick OAuth Config Editor. Do not pick Owner. Do not pick the broad Editor role. Your own account can stay Owner. That is separate from the role you give us.

    If Google says the address must be associated with an active Google Account, or if OAuth Config Editor does not appear in the role box, stop and email verify@velda.ai. Do not switch to Owner.

  6. Click Save. If Google asks to send an invitation, say yes. “Pending” is normal until we accept it.

Easy mistakes

  • Wrong project. Check the name in the top bar before you save.
  • Picking Owner, or the broad Editor role, for our email “to be safe”. Your own account can stay Owner or Editor. Our email should be OAuth Config Editor.
  • A typo in the email. Paste verify@velda.ai.

We will email you to remove us. The clicks are on the done-for-you page. You can remove us sooner any time. If you do that before we finish, the done-for-you refund exception applies.

If there is no Grant access button

You are probably signed in with an account that is not an owner of this project. Switch to the account that owns it. We will not ask you to hand that account’s password to us.
